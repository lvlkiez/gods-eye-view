# Tangmo case: source ledger for an evidence-linked spatial explorer

Checked **2 October 2026** (Asia/Bangkok). This is an editorial research ledger, not an independent forensic reconstruction or a court record. All clock times below are local Thai time. A timestamp reported by police from GPS/CCTV is a **police interpretation of evidence**, not access to the original GPS export or video. Do not turn these anchors into exact boat coordinates or a photorealistic replay without the original media, metadata, calibration and permission to use them.

### Material discrepancy to expose in the UI

S1 says “22:00” and reads as though the group **boarded/departed** then, followed by the fall. S2's later police GPS account says the boat moved from the dock at **16:49**, stopped at the restaurant at **18:24**, departed it at **20:08**, and was seen on the river after 21:00. Thus the S1 22:00 entry should be replaced with an explicit **early report, superseded/ambiguous wording** card, not reused as departure time. S2's **22:34:10** is an inferred fall time from police interpretation of video, not an exact sensor measurement. The S1 13:10 discovery time and S2 reference to roughly 13:00 should be shown as a small reported range or separate source entries rather than silently conflated.

## Proceedings/status as of 2 Oct 2026

1. Police presented a negligence-based case and their CCTV/GPS chronology on 26 Apr 2022; it was sent for prosecutorial review. [S2](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/314905). This is a prosecutorial theory, not a verdict.
2. On 10 May 2023, two defendants who had admitted charges received suspended sentences, according to [Thai PBS's 2026 retrospective](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/506692). The retrospective is a secondary account; verify judgment text before quoting sentence details in the product.
3. On 23 May 2025, the Nonthaburi Provincial Court acquitted three defendants on the negligent-death count and imposed a suspended sentence on another for separate offenses, per [Thai PBS's court report](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/352433). The same-day follow-up says the family intended to appeal. A [September 2026 interview](https://mgronline.com/entertainment/detail/9690000089092) quotes a family representative saying an appeal had been filed on 22 December 2025, but a court docket or resulting order was not verified; present filing as **reported by the representative**, not court-confirmed. [2025 follow-up](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/352450).
4. DSI's [7 Jan 2025 official note](https://www.dsi.go.th/th/Detail/0643f076e657fb2c8f0a63bf10a29a5e) specifically disavowed the public volunteer reenactment as its own work. Later [Thai PBS reporting](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/506692) says DSI investigated possible process/evidence irregularities, but these are allegations/inquiry matters, not established misconduct.
5. On 26 Aug 2026, DSI delivered 54 folders to NACC, confirmed by [FM91](https://www.fm91bkk.com/newsarticle/77990) and [PPTV](https://www.pptvhd36.com/news/%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A1/282253). FM91 reported DSI had found no prima facie section-157 offense and had ordered closure of its investigation on 5 Aug before the mandatory referral; a complainant quoted by PPTV disagreed that referral meant the matter was over. **No accessible NACC disposition or full DSI order was verified in this research.** Product wording: “ส่งเอกสารให้ ป.ป.ช. แล้ว; ผลการพิจารณาต่อยังไม่ยืนยันจากเอกสารทางการ.”

Avoid converting a complainant's allegation, a preliminary police interpretation, or a journalist's hypothesis into adjudicated fact. Do not state that the case is murder or identify a murderer. Also do not assert that all legal avenues are closed merely because the first-instance judgment exists; the later procedural record is incomplete here.

## Media and spatial-data use

- **Public source video**: Link to [Thai PBS's 26 Apr 2022 news segment](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/program/EveningNews/watch/EdqxRY) ([YouTube upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xpv4U2xqLEM)) as **news coverage about the police briefing**. The newsroom describes police use of an event-sequence video and immersive visualization; the segment itself is not verified original CCTV or the full press conference. No excerpt/timecode or reuse rights have been audited here. Check publisher permissions before embedding or reproducing frames.
- **Evidence gaps**: No released primary GPS track points, raw timestamped CCTV clips with provenance, source phone metadata, calibrated camera poses, measured vessel geometry, bathymetry/current observations, or 2022 historical river imagery were verified. These gaps rule out a defensible continuous vessel path, fall animation, historical photogrammetric model or speed measurement in this demo.
- **Map**: Satellite basemap can give present-day context with provider attribution. It cannot depict the river/shoreline precisely as on 24 Feb 2022. A synthetic route must not overlay contemporary satellite imagery as if it were measured; keep conceptual boat motion in an unmistakably schematic scene or replace it with named-landmark cards/camera flyovers.
- **Recommended evidence model**: Each UI item should carry `claim`, `timePrecision`, `sourceIds`, `statementDate`, `claimType` (`reported`, `police_interpretation`, `court_report`, `illustrative`), `spatialPrecision` (`place_name`, `area`, `unknown`), and a visible limitation. Where sources disagree, display both cards and a discrepancy note. Never infer coordinates from a news place name.
