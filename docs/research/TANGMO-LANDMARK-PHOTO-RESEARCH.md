# Tangmo landmark photo research

## Finding

No freely reusable photo was verified as depicting **Pibulsongkram 1 Pier**. Wikimedia Commons search results identify nearby pier photographs as **Nonthaburi (Phibul 3)**; that is a different pier and must not be captioned or used as Pibulsongkram 1. The Commons file [Nonthaburi-Pibul 3 Pier 2.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nonthaburi-Pibul_3_Pier_2.jpg) is explicitly described as Phibul 3, geotagged at 13.842358, 100.491128, dated 31 December 2017, authored by Slleong, and released CC0. Its license is reusable, but its subject identity makes it unsuitable as a Pibulsongkram 1 photo.

Official identity references:

- The Thai Marine Department's [express-boat pier inventory](https://md.go.th/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/description_express_boat57.pdf) names “ท่าพิบูลสงคราม 1” and lists Wat Pak Nam as a nearby place.
- The Office of Transport and Traffic Policy and Planning's [transport-sector action plan](https://www.otp.go.th/uploads/tiny_uploads/PDF/2568-06/POTP-25062025-ActionPlanforTransport_Eng.pdf) separately names the Pibulsongkram Pier 1 project in Nonthaburi.
- Longdo's [approved N25 Pibul (Phibun) Songkhram 1 place record](https://map.longdo.com/poilist/31?page=8) identifies the pier and its Soi Phibun Songkhram 3 address. This is a mapping record, not photo-license evidence.

## Honest Pibulsongkram 1 visual

Use the existing live Esri satellite basemap centered on the app's Pibulsongkram 1 coordinate as **satellite context**. Label it as map imagery (including the provider and imagery-date caveat); it is not a ground-level pier photograph, incident-night evidence, or proof of the pier's historical appearance. If the interface needs a photo tile, use a neutral illustrative pier rendering clearly marked as illustrative instead of substituting a Phibul 3 or bridge photo.
